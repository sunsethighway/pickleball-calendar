# THE DAN OF MY DREAMS — poster brief

A Pixar-style one-sheet for a fictional animated feature, made from a photo of
Dan and James at a pickleball meet.

## Logline

James can't stop dreaming about Dan. Every night, the moment the floodlights go
out, the gazebo roof peels back into open sky and Dan is up there — enormous,
backlit, delighted with himself — surfing the clouds on a pickleball paddle.

## Cast

| Character | Who they are in the photo | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **James** — the dreamer | Right of frame | Late thirties, tousled sandy-blond quiff, neat ginger-blond beard and moustache, warm blue eyes, white tee, black sports shorts, easy grin. **Lean and wiry — a runner's build, narrow waist, slender arms and legs. Not stocky.** |
| **Dan** — the dream | Left of frame | Slim, short mid-brown hair, thin-rimmed tinted glasses, enormous beaming smile, heather-grey tee, light blue shorts |

## Title treatment

- **Studio bar (top):** SUFFOLK PICKLE STUDIOS
- **Title:** THE DAN OF MY DREAMS
- **Tagline:** HE'S NOT IN HIS HEAD. HE'S IN HIS REM CYCLE.
- **Footer:** SUMMER 2026 — ONLY IN DREAMS
- Chunky rounded 3D animated-title face, soft bevels, gold-to-cream gradient,
  gentle outer glow, subtle drop shadow. Set on three lines with DREAMS largest.
- No real studio wordmarks or logos — the typography evokes the genre, the
  branding is invented.

## Composition

- **2:3 vertical one-sheet.**
- **Lower third:** James asleep sideways in a folding camping chair under a white
  pop-up gazebo at dusk, cheek squashed, blissful sleepy smile, hugging a green
  pickleball paddle like a teddy bear. Golden `z z z` curling upward.
- **Upper two thirds:** the gazebo canopy dissolves into a swirling twilight
  dream-sky. Dan floats there larger than life, backlit, riding a paddle like a
  surfboard, arms flung wide, a cape of festoon string-lights streaming behind
  him, laughing.
- **Dream furniture:** neon-yellow perforated pickleballs drifting like little
  glowing planets, a staircase of floating paddles winding up through the cloud
  bank, fireflies, a big soft moon behind Dan, god-rays, a glowing pickleball
  court far below.

## Palette

Deep twilight teal and indigo sky against warm gold and neon-yellow key light.
Polished, luminous, heart-warming.

## Companion scenes

Three film stills in the same world, 3:2 landscape, no type on any of them:

1. **The hug** — a huge bear hug on court at golden hour, both mid-laugh with
   eyes squeezed shut, paddles dropped at their feet, a pickleball rolling away.
2. **The paddle tap** — the classic post-point tap at the net, low hero angle,
   a burst of sparkle where the paddle faces meet, ball frozen mid-air behind.
3. **The ride** — both astride a planet-sized neon-yellow pickleball hurtling
   through the dream sky, James steering, Dan hanging on and laughing, stardust
   trailing behind.

the summer-sky-and-treeline backdrop and the pickleball setting are all lifted
