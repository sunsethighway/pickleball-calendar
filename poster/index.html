The Dan
of My Dreams

He's not in his head. He's in his REM cycle.

The poster

James asleep in a camping chair under the gazebo, hugging a paddle like a teddy bear, while the canopy above him dissolves into a twilight dream-sky — and Dan soars through it on a paddle, a cape of festoon lights streaming behind him. Two takes on the same brief.

The hug

Golden hour on court, both mid-laugh with their eyes squeezed shut, paddles dropped at their feet, a pickleball rolling away.

The paddle tap

The classic post-point tap at the net, low hero angle, a burst of sparkle where the paddle faces meet.

The ride

Both astride a planet-sized neon-yellow pickleball hurtling through the dream sky, James steering, Dan hanging on and laughing.
