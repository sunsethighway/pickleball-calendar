The Dan
of My Dreams

He's not in his head. He's in his REM cycle.

The poster

James asleep in a camping chair under the gazebo, hugging a Boomstik like a teddy bear, while the canopy above him dissolves into a twilight dream-sky — and Dan soars through it on a paddle, a cape of festoon lights streaming behind him. Two takes on the same brief.

The promo

Ten seconds, cut for a phone. Four beats lifted from the running order of RallyPoint's own Short and rebuilt in the dream world.

Chest badge, pushing in on the Rally Point script and tilting up to a grin. A rally, low and fast, James lunging to dink while Dan covers behind. The whole club, a wide of players of all ages across the courts. The two of them, shoulder to shoulder down the lens, paddle tap and a thumbs up.

The hug

Golden hour on court, both mid-laugh with their eyes squeezed shut, paddles dropped at their feet, a pickleball rolling away.

The paddle tap

The classic post-point tap at the net, low hero angle, a burst of sparkle where the paddle faces meet.

The ride

Both astride a planet-sized neon-yellow pickleball hurtling through the dream sky, James steering, Dan hanging on and laughing.

Rendered on Higgsfield with nano_banana_pro at 2K, and served from the live press-kit site where the files are committed into the project rather than hot-linked from the render CDN. Both players swing a Selkirk LABS Project Boomstik and wear the RallyPoint badge; the real logo and the real product shot were used as reference art. Suffolk Pickle Studios is invented; no real studio branding is used.
