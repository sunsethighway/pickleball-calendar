# THE DAN OF MY DREAMS — gallery

> **Live press-kit site: https://dan-of-my-dreams.higgsfield.app**
> The images are baked into that site, not hot-linked, so it stands on its own.

A Pixar-style one-sheet and three companion stills for a fictional animated
feature, built from a photo of Dan and James at a pickleball meet.

> **James** — lean, sandy-blond quiff, ginger-blond beard, white tee.
> **Dan** — slim, tinted glasses, enormous grin, heather-grey tee.

Rendered on Higgsfield with `nano_banana_pro` at 2K. Brief in
[`the-dan-of-my-dreams-BRIEF.md`](the-dan-of-my-dreams-BRIEF.md), exact prompt in
[`the-dan-of-my-dreams-PROMPT.txt`](the-dan-of-my-dreams-PROMPT.txt).

---

## The poster

**HE'S NOT IN HIS HEAD. HE'S IN HIS REM CYCLE.**

James asleep in a camping chair under the gazebo, hugging a paddle like a teddy
bear, while the canopy above him dissolves into a twilight dream-sky — and Dan
soars through it on a paddle, cape of festoon lights streaming behind him.

## The scenes

### The hug

Golden hour on court, both mid-laugh with their eyes squeezed shut, paddles
dropped at their feet, a pickleball rolling away.

<img src="https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/user_3GUPCn4sC5wUC6rVbuYgnFTPXut/hf_20260727_100559_2d5ee7c5-31ed-41ec-905e-9bece7751522.png" width="720" alt="Dan and James hugging on court at golden hour">

### The paddle tap

The classic post-point tap at the net, low hero angle, a burst of sparkle where
the paddle faces meet.

<img src="https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/user_3GUPCn4sC5wUC6rVbuYgnFTPXut/hf_20260727_100611_22f3fdc4-53ff-4ef8-af77-b396de3caa98.png" width="720" alt="Dan and James tapping paddles at the net">

### The ride

Both astride a planet-sized neon-yellow pickleball hurtling through the dream
sky, James steering, Dan hanging on and laughing.

<img src="https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/user_3GUPCn4sC5wUC6rVbuYgnFTPXut/hf_20260727_100618_c4b129f8-7bd8-4cad-97cf-87b74b8796b5.png" width="720" alt="Dan and James riding a giant pickleball through the clouds">
